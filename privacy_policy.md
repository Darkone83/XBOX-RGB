# XBOX RGB — Privacy Policy
_Last updated: 2025-11-16_

This Privacy Policy is written to comply with the requirements of the **Apple App Store**, **Google Play Store**, and global privacy standards such as **GDPR**, **COPPA**, and **CCPA**.

---

## 1. Introduction
XBOX RGB (“the App”) respects your privacy.  
This application does **not** collect, store, process, transmit, or share any personal or non‑personal data.

---

## 2. Information We Collect
**We collect: _No Data_.**

Specifically, the App does **not** collect or access:
- Personal information  
- Device identifiers  
- Photos, media, or files  
- Usage data or analytics  
- Diagnostics or telemetry  
- Location data  
- Advertising identifiers  
- Network information  
- Any other user or device data  

The App does not create user accounts and does not require login of any kind.

---

## 3. Data Sharing
Since the App does not collect any data, it also **does not share any data** with:
- Third parties  
- Advertisers  
- Analytics providers  
- External servers  
- Cloud services  

No data is ever transmitted off the device.

---

## 4. Local Operation Only
All features of XBOX RGB (such as device control, configuration, and Bluetooth communication) operate **entirely on-device**.  
Bluetooth communication is used solely for interacting with compatible hardware and is never logged, stored, or transmitted externally.

---

## 5. Third‑Party Services
The App does **not** use:
- Analytics SDKs  
- Advertising SDKs  
- Data collection libraries  
- Third‑party cloud APIs  

---

## 6. Children’s Privacy
Because XBOX RGB collects **no data from any user**, it is fully compliant with COPPA, GDPR‑K, and other child privacy protections.

The App is safe for all ages.

---

## 7. Your Rights
Because the App stores no personal data:
- There is no data to request access to  
- There is no data to modify  
- There is no data to delete  
- There is no data to opt‑out of sharing  

---

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
If this policy is updated, changes will be posted in the project repository or within the app.  
No changes will reduce user privacy without explicit notice.
